SaaS-Adjacent Productization2023-2026

Turning generic branding into packaged products to unlock a scalable SaaS-style motion

BUSINESS CONTEXT: Wgentech's marketing focused on branding the company broadly rather than promoting a specific offering, giving the team no defined target to sell against. With every deal scoped case-by-case, there was no repeatable product to market, price, or measure - the core blocker to building a SaaS-style growth engine.

Strategic Decisions

  • Proposed A/B testing productized service packages (SME/Growing Brands/Enterprise) against the existing generic-branding approach
  • Tested conversion, sales cycle, and lead quality as the three success criteria for productization
  • Defined fixed-scope packages by company size to give Marketing a specific, promotable target instead of broad positioning
  • Kept Enterprise flexible pending test results, since deal scope varied too much to standardize upfront

Execution

  • Packaged SME and Growing Brand tiers into fixed-scope offers with clear pricing and deliverables
  • Ran A/B test: packaged offers vs. generic branding across conversion rate, sales cycle length, and MQL/SQL quality
  • Tracked CAC payback and MRR growth as SaaS-style efficiency metrics, since scope became standardized and repeatable
  • Based on test results, kept Enterprise sales-led - deal-specific scope and cycle length made it unsuitable for productization

Mobile App Activation Optimization2021-2023

Diagnosing & closing activation blind spot for Capichi's mobile app

BUSINESS CONTEXT: A budget was split evenly across Meta and Google, but the team had never built activation funnel tracking. With no visibility into where users dropped between install and first order, every campaign optimization was a guess rather than a data-driven decision.

Strategic Decisions

  • Prioritized event-level funnel tracking before touching any campaign budget
  • Redefined "Activation" as first order placed, not sign-up, to remove a vanity metric
  • Held budget split steady during diagnosis to isolate the real bottleneck
  • Aligned Product & Design around funnel drop-off as a shared problem

Execution

  • Built full-funnel event tracking (install → sign up → browse → first order) via AppsFlyer & Firebase
  • Ran full-funnel analysis with Product & Design to pinpoint the highest drop-off step
  • Reallocated Meta/Google budget based on attribution data once baseline was established
  • Targeted email, in-app messaging and push notifications to the exact step users were stuck at

D2C Website CROFreelance: 2025-2026

Turning a mobile-first traffic base into a converting revenue channel for a fashion brand

BUSINESS CONTEXT: With $1M annual GMV driven mainly by offline and paid channels, the brand's website converted at just 0.25% site-wide, despite 85% of traffic coming from mobile. Only 2.3% of sessions reached Add-to-Cart, signaling a fundamental mobile experience gap rather than a traffic problem.

Strategic Decisions

  • Prioritized mobile UX fixes over acquisition spend, since 85% of traffic was already mobile-native
  • Reframed the funnel around Session → ATC as the primary diagnostic metric, not just CVR
  • Built a lifecycle layer (Email + SMS) to capture value beyond first purchase, not just drive new traffic
  • Treated retention as a growth lever equal to acquisition, given heavy reliance on offline/paid for new demand

Execution

  • Audited and rebuilt mobile checkout and product page flows to close the Session-to-ATC gap
  • Redesigned mobile navigation, page speed, and add-to-cart friction points across the funnel
  • Launched Email + SMS flows for abandonment improvement, retention and win-back, targeting repeat purchase from existing customers
  • Iterated site-wide CVR improvements over 6 months, validated against cohort data

SEO & Content Strategy2018-2021

Realigning organic content from mass-market intent to a high-value luxury audience.

BUSINESS CONTEXT: European Luxury Travel brand's website had organic traffic, but it was pulling the wrong intent. Visitors searched for budget and mass-market travel rather than luxury European experiences. Volume existed; qualified demand didn't, with booking inquiry conversion sitting at just 0.4%.

Strategic Decisions

  • Shifted keyword strategy from volume-driven to intent-driven, targeting luxury-specific search terms over generic travel queries
  • Rebuilt content pillars around EU luxury traveler decision points (destination, experience, exclusivity) instead of broad travel topics
  • Prioritized qualified lead quality over raw traffic growth as the primary success metric
  • Committed to a 6–12 month SEO horizon, aligning stakeholders on content authority as a compounding asset

Execution

  • Audited existing content and search rankings to identify mass-market keyword cannibalization
  • Rewrote and restructured blog/SEO content around luxury-intent keywords and EU audience search behavior
  • Built topic clusters and internal linking to establish topical authority in the luxury travel niche
  • Tracked organic traffic and booking inquiry conversion in parallel to validate audience quality
